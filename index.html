Privacy Policy for STxzori Calculator

Effective Date: September 23, 2026

STxzori Calculator respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how STxzori Calculator handles information when you use the application.

Information We Access

STxzori Calculator may request access to your device microphone when you use the voice input feature.

The microphone is used to recognize your spoken calculation and convert your speech into text so that the calculator can perform the requested calculation.

STxzori Calculator does not require you to create an account and does not require your name, phone number, address, contacts, photos, or location.

Voice Input

When you use the voice input feature, your speech may be processed by the speech recognition service provided by your Android device or its service provider in order to convert speech into text.

STxzori Calculator does not intentionally store voice recordings on its own servers.

Calculation History and Memory

Calculation history and calculator memory are used to provide calculator functionality.

These features are stored locally on your device and are not intentionally transmitted by STxzori Calculator to a personal server.

You can clear available calculator data using the app settings or by clearing the application data from your device.

Data Sharing

STxzori Calculator does not sell your personal information.

STxzori Calculator does not intentionally share your personal information with third parties.

Third-party services used by Android for speech recognition may process voice input according to their own privacy policies.

Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information handled by the application. However, no electronic storage or transmission method can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Data Retention and Deletion

STxzori Calculator does not maintain a user account or a personal database containing user information.

Locally stored calculator history and memory can be cleared through the application or by clearing the application data from your Android device.

Children

STxzori Calculator is not specifically designed to collect personal information from children.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when necessary. Any updated version will be made available on this webpage.
